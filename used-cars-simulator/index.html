🛠️ Why "Tinsmith" Rust Removal Is Mandatory

On the in-game map, the Body Shop is labeled as "Tinsmith" in the southwest quadrant. Spray painting over a rusty wreck does not remove the rust status! Leaving chassis rust untreated causes buyers to slash their bids by 80% to 90% (a $5,500 car will only get $400–$700 offers). Either pay the Tinsmith percentage fee or use the Disc Sander from the Hardware Store (added in Update 28).

📈 The -10% / +10% Negotiation Formula

Spend your first 4 Skill Points in the Negotiation tree to unlock 10% Lower Purchase Price and 10% Higher Sale Price. Always inspect every damaged part on a seller's car first to watch the asking price above their head drop, then offer 10% below that number. When selling, sleep in your shed until a high offer arrives, tow the car to the buyer, and multiply their offer by 1.10!

📦 Cargo Delivery & Cross-Engine Swaps

Only 3 vehicles support internal package delivery side quests from the Port and Supermarket (Update 29): D-1CK, Burrito Bison, and Gravel Titan. Furthermore, engine mounts are unrestricted (Update 11) — you can pull a supercar motor out of a Feetari F420 and drop it straight into a starter Piat Funto!
