Complete By-Product Recycling & Sink Matrix

How to clear every hazardous by-product so your primary factories never stall:

1. Wood Waste (By-Product) Source: Woodcutter (5s cycle)

Best Sink: Feed into Compost Heap (produces 3x Compost for Tree Nursery & Terraforming). Avoid burning Wood Waste in Burner Generator early on, as Compost requires huge quantities of Wood Waste!

2. Soot (By-Product) Source: Burner Generator (15s), Ore Smelter (5s)

Best Sink: Store in dedicated Storage Shed (batches of 20) or By-Product Yard; export or process via Chemical/Cleaner tech chain.

3. Slag (By-Product) Source: Metal Smelter (Steel/Glass), Toolsmith (Tool)

Best Sink: Buffer in Warehouse / By-Product Yard next to industrial smelting district so Steel and Tool lines never pause.

4. Sludge (By-Product) Source: Cement Factory, Fuel Generator

Best Sink: Route directly to Sludge Dehydrator (requires Power, 2 workers) to convert hazardous Sludge back into clean Water!

5. Goo (By-Product) Source: Electronics Plant, Fuel Plant, Destressor

Best Sink: Store in Warehouse / Space Port or process in late-game Hazardous facilities.

Beyond These Stars Settlement Optimization Guide & Mechanics Reference

1. Early Production Ratios (2s vs 5s vs 15s)

In Beyond These Stars, a single Mine operates on a 2-second work cycle (extracting Stone, Iron Ore, Copper Ore, or Sand), whereas an Ore Smelter takes 5 seconds per Iron or Copper ingot. One Mine easily sustains an Ore Smelter plus construction stone demand. Meanwhile, the Burner Generator runs on a 15-second work cycle and can power multiple early buildings (Ore Smelter, Metal Smelter, Toolsmith).

2. Avoiding Hidden Adjacency Penalties

Never cluster identical production buildings without checking adjacency rules. Placing two Woodcutters touching reduces production speed by -20%. Placing two Ore Smelters touching inflicts -30% storage and -1 workplace. Keep Huts away from Water Pumps (-20% water pump speed) and instead pair each Hut with 2 Potato Patches (+20% Happiness, or +40% with the early Happiness Perk).

3. Unlocking the House & Mid-Game Tech

To unlock the House (7 Peeps capacity), your settlement needs a School (unlocked after Hut) and enough Peep population—up to 30 Huts, or fewer if you use the 3x Hut cluster adjacency (+1 housing slot per Hut, trading off -20% food energy recovery).
